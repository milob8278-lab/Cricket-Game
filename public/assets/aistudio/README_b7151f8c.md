## Expanded feature specifications

7. `07_COMPLETE_GAME_FEATURE_SPECIFICATION.md` — Detailed feature catalog for batting, bowling, fielding, rules, game modes, 3D characters, stadiums, weather, scoreboards, commentary, offline/online, settings, content and QA.
8. `08_FULLSCREEN_TOUCH_AND_ANIMATION_SPEC.md` — Full-screen landscape UI, batting/bowling/fielding touch controls, multi-touch behavior, layout editor, 3D rig and animation requirements.

## Agreed product baseline

- Android-first; Unity 6 LTS family + URP + C# recommended.
- Semi-realistic fictional 3D players.
- Active players stay 3D in Lite mode; Lite reduces model/render workload.
- 4GB RAM baseline target, to be proven on physical devices.
- Full-screen landscape touch-first match experience.
- Urdu and English.
- Offline-first; online services optional and staged.
- Core gameplay free and unlocked; no ads during active play.

## Important note

These files are developer-facing specifications. They describe the intended game and its acceptance criteria; they are not a compiled Unity project or a completed playable APK. Items marked recommended/open decision must be finalized during pre-production.
